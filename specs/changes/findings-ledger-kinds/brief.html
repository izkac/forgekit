Findings ledger kinds

TL;DR

The open-findings number has been mixing bugs, debt, trade-offs, ideas and process notes — almost all labelled major by default. After this change the headline means open bugs, findings can point at their root cause, and a fix that regressed twice can block finishing that subject again.

What you'll get

  • Every new finding must declare a kind (bug|debt|tradeoff|idea|process) and a severity — no silent defaults.
  • forge finding list and forge status show bugs by default; other kinds stay in the ledger but off the headline.
  • Closing a root cause lists its open dependents so they get re-checked (they are not auto-closed).
  • Reopened findings are first-class: sorted and marked in the list, named in status; a finding reopened twice can refuse forge phase done on that change unless explicitly waived.

How it works

flowchart LR
    A[forge finding add
--kind --severity] --> L[(.forge/findings.jsonl)]
    L --> B[list / status
bugs only]
    L --> C[resolve root]
    C -->|prints| D[Re-check dependents]
    L --> E[reopen]
    E -->|reopenCount ≥ 2| F[phase done gate]

What changes for you

  • Agents filing findings must pass --kind and --severity or the command fails with the allowed values.
  • forge finding list --all-kinds shows everything; the default list footer says how many non-bugs are hidden.
  • New: forge finding link, forge finding reopen, and --reopen-waived on phase done.

Out of scope

THOROUGH_RE corpus (W5), surfacing bugs on forge new (W6), and skill-doc guardrails (W7) are follow-up changes after this schema lands.

Success

  • openFindings.count is a single-digit bug count after backfill, and it is the number people mean when they say "open findings".
  • Resolving a linked root always names its open dependents.
  • F11 / F13 show as reopened in status, not as ordinary majors.
