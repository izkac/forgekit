Making the brainstorm contract real

TL;DR

Until now the improved brainstorm was all instructions — nothing checked the agent actually kept its promises. This change adds teeth: Forge refuses to move from brainstorm to planning unless the Assumptions ledger was actually written, answers worth keeping get offered a permanent home instead of dying with the session, and Forge starts measuring whether designs hold up after approval.

What you'll get

  • An enforced ledger: after a brainstorm, moving on to planning fails unless the design notes contain an Assumptions section. A recorded waiver (--notes-waived "reason") exists for the rare legitimate skip; sessions that never brainstormed are unaffected.
  • Answers that stick: when a brainstorm closes, answers that are really standing project rules ("jobs always go through the queue") get offered — with your consent — a permanent home in your glossary, agent rules, or decision records, so future sessions stop re-asking.
  • A churn signal: every time the plan brief has to be re-stamped after implementation started (meaning the approved spec was edited mid-build), Forge counts it. forge analyze reports which sessions churned and how rich their assumption ledgers were — a direct read on interview quality. Grades and scores don't change.

How it works

The gate joins the existing family of transition checks (the same mechanism that refuses implementation without a stamped brief) and fires only when the session actually went through brainstorm. The churn counter piggybacks on brief stamping, which already fingerprints the spec files. The new numbers ride along in the session ledger that forge analyze already reads. An end-to-end harness check drives the real CLI through refuse, pass, and waiver paths.

What changes for you

  • If an agent skips the Assumptions ledger, forge phase plan now stops it with a message saying exactly what to write.
  • At the end of a brainstorm you may be asked "should this answer become a permanent rule?" — nothing is written without your yes.
  • forge analyze gains one summary line and richer per-session rows. No new required steps.

Risks and open questions

  • A session upgraded mid-flight could hit the gate without notes — the refusal message and the waiver cover it.
  • Re-stamps are a proxy for spec churn, not a perfect measure; good enough to spot patterns, not to grade individual sessions (deliberately kept out of scoring).

Out of scope

  • Any change to scoring or grades.
  • Judge-panel approach generation and prototype-as-interview-move (other suggestions, not picked).

Work overview

  • The plan gate with waiver + tests (1 task).
  • Churn counters, ledger fields, analyze surfacing + tests (2 tasks).
  • Skill/phase-doc updates and the end-to-end gate check (2 tasks).
