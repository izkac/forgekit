Cleanup plan-phase sessions

Finding F48 · change cleanup-plan-phase

TL;DR

Bare forge cleanup will no longer delete an unfinished triage/plan session whose change directory still exists under specs/changes/<name>/. That change dir is treated as held work, same as files inside the session directory.

What you'll get

  • Plan-phase sessions with a live change keep their session record (phase history, host binding) until you finish or explicitly delete.
  • Escape hatch unchanged: forge cleanup --include-unfinished --session <id>.

Out of scope

  • Analyze-report honesty (F64–F69) — separate change.
Only active change dirs count. An archived change under changes/archive/ does not protect the session.
