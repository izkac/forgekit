A faster, sharper brainstorm interview

TL;DR

Forge's brainstorm phase currently asks you questions one at a time, with no recommendations and no clear end. After this change it interviews in rounds: every question that can be asked right now arrives together, each with a recommended answer, and the session ends only when nothing is left open or silently assumed. A ten-message interrogation becomes two or three rounds — or one reply of "all recommended".

What you'll get

  • Questions arrive batched in numbered rounds instead of one per message; follow-up questions that depend on your answers come in the next round.
  • Every question carries a recommended answer. Reply "all recommended" to accept a whole round, or answer just the ones you disagree with.
  • You are never asked something the agent could look up in the codebase — it investigates in the background and only brings you real decisions.
  • The design you approve now includes an Assumptions section: every default the agent adopted without asking is listed there for you to review, so nothing is silently assumed.
  • Faster paces stay meaningful: at lower brainstorm depth, unasked branches become recommended assumptions you approve with the design instead of questions you never saw.

How it works

The interview is modeled as a decision tree. At any moment some questions are ready to ask (their prerequisites are settled) and some are blocked behind them. The agent asks the whole ready set in one round, waits for your answers, recomputes what that unblocks, and repeats. Fact-finding never enters the rounds — it runs as background exploration. The interview is over when no question remains ready or blocked, and every noted default has either been answered or written into the design's Assumptions section.

What changes for you

  • Brainstorm conversations look different: rounds of numbered questions with recommendations, separated by dividers.
  • You gain the "all recommended" shortcut.
  • Design docs you approve gain an Assumptions section — worth a skim, it holds the decisions you were not asked about.
  • No new commands, settings, or files; pace names are unchanged.

Risks and open questions

  • This edits agent instructions, not code — the benefit depends on the agent following them; there is no mechanical enforcement of round structure.
  • Three documents describe the interview; a consistency sweep keeps them aligned, but future edits must keep them in step.

Out of scope

  • The other researched improvements (scenario red-teaming, glossary/ADR pass, spike classification, questionnaire hand-offs) — deferred.
  • Re-syncing the skill to other machines (existing forgekit install flow).
